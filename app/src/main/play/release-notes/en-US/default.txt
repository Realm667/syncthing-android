ES-DE SafeSync 2.1.4.12
- RETRY now clears stale conflict entries only when their local conflict files no longer exist.
- Real conflicts remain protected by the existing single-file and batch resolution workflow.
- Adds POWER OFF DEVICE to the completed IDLE screen with confirmation and safety checks.
- Uses supported system/vendor shutdown handling where available and an explicit root fallback.
- Unsupported devices remain safely idle and receive a clear permission explanation.
